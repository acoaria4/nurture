# Nurture — A place for everyday care

## Visual direction
The object is the identity: a cream tin, metallic gold, botanical linework. A deep mineral-green opening pairs an oversized lowercase serif masthead with an uninterrupted product cutout. Quiet cream editorial spreads, precise rules, asymmetry, and an oxblood accent bring the campaign away from conventional wellness templates. Cormorant Garamond is the display face, Manrope is the reading and utility face. Mobile has its own stacked composition, compact masthead, and full-width product study.

Sequence: product-first hero → intention → interactive object study → two material details → cinematic campaign strip → honest development FAQ → closing brand statement.

Motion: a short hero sequence; headings and selected media enter once; a modest campaign-image scroll transform. GSAP / ScrollTrigger owns the choreography; Lenis is the sole optional smooth-scroll engine on desktop. Native scrolling on touch and reduced motion. No pinning or blocking intro. No live Three.js: existing CGI views already communicate the object clearly.

Primary action: Explore the concept. There is no configured email service, verified contact address, launch date or privacy policy, so the current site does not collect personal data or simulate signups. A future waitlist requires an actual service and consent copy.

Provenance: copied, existing brand-supplied marks; existing CGI concept renders; existing AI-generated campaign and lifestyle concepts. No new product claims or ingredient/benefit assumptions. Final artwork and packaging dimensions are not approved.

Selected design skills: build-awwwards-quality-sites and editorial-tech.
