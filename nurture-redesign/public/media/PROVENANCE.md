# Concept media provenance

All visual assets are copies or delivery derivatives of files already present in the parent Nurture repository.

- `hero.webp`, `hero-small.webp`: alpha-trimmed/resized versions of `assets/product-photos/v2/nurture-angle-transparent-v2.png`.
- `front-cutout.webp`: alpha-trimmed/resized `nurture-front-transparent-v2.png`.
- `artwork-detail.webp`: a close crop of `nurture-front-v2.png` showing the artwork.
- `open-lid.webp`, `top.webp`, `lid-detail.webp`, `front.webp`, `three-quarter.webp`: copies from `assets/web/`, rendered from the existing concept tin model. The open tin is intentionally empty; no contents have been inferred.
- `campaign.webp`, `campaign-640.webp`, `campaign-1000.webp`: existing AI-generated product campaign concept.
- `morning.webp`: existing AI-generated lifestyle study; the depicted woman is not a customer or endorser.
- `trayn-symbol.webp`, `trayn-wordmark.webp`: delivery encodings of the supplied TRAYN logo artwork. Their alpha silhouettes are tinted with CSS; the marks are not redrawn.

The original reference provides product identity, the “Everyday Nutrition for Every Woman” positioning, the “To Feel Strong. To Live Well. Every Day.” vision and a 400g pack reference. Artwork benefit icons or nutrient categories are not treated as independently verified claims. The final formulation, claims and production packaging need approved source material.

See the parent repository's `assets/ASSET-NOTES.md` and `assets/product-photos/v2/README.md` for source prompts and model details.
